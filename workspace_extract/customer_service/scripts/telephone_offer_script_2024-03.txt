TELEPHONE OFFER CALL SCRIPT (counter, rev. March 2024)
=========================================================

Use when phoning an applicant about a new offer. The letter still
governs — the call is a courtesy; the window runs from the LETTER
date (10 days covered / 14 days open). Say so plainly.

1. Identify yourself: "Chaldale Harbor District moorage counter,
   this is [name]."
2. Confirm you are speaking with the applicant of record. Never
   discuss rank or offers with anyone else.
3. "A slip has come up and we are mailing you an offer today:
   slip [ID], [basin], [size] feet, [covered/open], at [rate]
   per foot-month."
4. "Your response window is [10/14] calendar days from the date
   on the letter — not from today. We need your acceptance in
   writing or in person at the counter."
5. If they hesitate: "You can decline without losing your place.
   A second decline moves you to the bottom of the list unless
   you take the retainer option — I can explain that if useful."
6. If they mention a new boat: "Before you accept, we should
   update your particulars. If the length has changed, we will
   need a measurement certificate or a haul-out measurement
   sheet before the new length counts — that has been the rule
   since Resolution 2022-06."
7. Close: note the call in the day log with the time and your
   initials, and drop the letter the same day.

DO NOT: quote anyone else's position on the list, promise a
date for the next offer, or agree to hold a slip past its
window. Those are the three calls that come back to us.
