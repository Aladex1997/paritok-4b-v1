# KVL Camps — Aquatics Credential Authority Review

## Prompt

**To:** Joseph R. Almeida, Camp Director, Corrigwell
**From:** Priya Natarajan, Director of Risk and Compliance
**Date:** July 15, 2026
**Re:** Credential authority gaps at Corrigwell — pre-close compliance check

---

Priya,

With season close on August 20 coming fast and the Bureau's CAP-2026-01 response deadline on July 30, I need a complete picture of credential authority at Corrigwell before we file anything.

As of the reporting cutoff of July 15, 2026, I need you to cross-reference our credential records against our actual staffing to answer one question: **Which staff members currently working at Corrigwell hold credentials that are current but do NOT authorize them for the duties they are performing, specifically the authority to certify swim-ability assessments and sign classification rosters?**

Here's what I need in your deliverable:

1. **Staff authority audit** — For every waterfront staff member currently assigned to Corrigwell for the 2026 season, determine whether their highest-credential track authorizes them for:
   - Deep-water lifeguarding supervision
   - Swim-ability assessment and classification sign-off (the authority to certify a camper's band on the classification roster)
   - Waterpark or attraction lifeguarding

   Use the credential-track authorization matrix as the controlling reference. A credential can be current and still not authorize a duty — currency and authority are different things.

2. **Authorization gap register** — Produce a table listing every staff member where the credential they hold for a duty does not match what the authorization matrix says is required for that duty. Include: staff name, assigned site and role, credential code(s) held, credential status (current/expired/grace), and the specific duty they are NOT authorized for.

3. **Immediate risk flags** — Call out any staff member who:
   - Is currently working as a lifeguard or assessment authorizer at Corrigwell without the matrix-required credential for that duty
   - Has a credential that expired within the reporting period and has not been renewed
   - Holds an Aquatics Supervisor Certificate (AQS) and is being relied upon for assessment authority (note: AQS is NOT assessment-authorized per the matrix, Rev. 2021 onward)

**Format:** Deliver as a .xlsx workbook with three tabs named exactly: `Staff Authority Audit`, `Authorization Gap Register`, `Immediate Risk Flags`. Each tab must contain headers in row 1 and be sorted by staff name A-Z.

**Constraints:**
- Do not infer credentials from role titles — verify each against the certification files under Credential Records/Corrigwell/2026/
- Do not use credentials from other sites (Bluegill Point, Heron Landing, etc.) for Corrigwell staff — if a staff member transferred, note it but evaluate only their current Corrigwell assignment
- Do not assume CPR/AED components have the same validity as the lifeguarding certificate they ride inside — CPR/AED is tracked at 12 months, not 24 months, per the Credential Validity Quick Reference
- Do not include water quality, incident, or ratio data in this deliverable — scope the finding to credential authority only
- The authorization matrix is the controlling authority, not the Swim Classification and Authorization Standard narrative — if there is a conflict between the two, the matrix governs

This needs to be clean before the July 30 deadline. The Bureau findings unit will compare this against our CAP response.
