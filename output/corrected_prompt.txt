GOVERNING AUTHORITIES

All determinations must be grounded in the following governing documents:
- Standing Order 15-3: Governs skip file requirements. Section 3.2 requires a contemporaneous cover sheet plus a supporting record assembled on the day the slip was worked. Section 3.3 requires supporting records to be created contemporaneously (on the day the slip was worked). Section 5.1 names the skip event register as a District record. Per Appeal 2018-3, the burden of proof shifts to the District when a skip is not evidenced; if the District cannot produce a contemporaneous support record, the skip is upheld for the applicant.
- Resolution 2022-06: Governs billing rate adoption (effective August 1, 2022), billable length formula, and amendment verification requirements. Section 1.1 sets post-adoption rates at $10.40/foot-month (covered) and $7.55/foot-month (open). Section 4.3 requires amendments to be supported by certification. Section 1.4 governs billable length.
- Tariff Resolution 2019-14: Pre-adoption tariff framework (effective March 12, 2019). Rates: $9.85/foot-month (covered) and $7.10/foot-month (open). Used only for periods before August 1, 2022.
- Appeal 2018-3 Decision: Legal precedent establishing that the District bears the burden of proof for missed skips unless contemporaneous same-day proof is provided.

BILLING POLICY

Billable length = MAX(rounded-up check-in LOA from applicant register, posted slip length from slip inventory). For July 2026 (post-adoption), all recurring moorage transactions use post-adoption rates. Post-adoption rates: $10.40/foot-month for covered basins, $7.55/foot-month for open basins. Pre-adoption rates (before August 1, 2022): $9.85/foot-month covered, $7.10/foot-month open. Billable basis is foot-month (length in feet multiplied by months billed).

Contemporaneous proof of eligibility means a cover sheet plus a supporting record (measurement certificate or equivalent) assembled on the day the slip was worked, as required by Standing Order 15-3 s.3.2-3.3. Check-in measurements refer to vessel length measurements documented in measurement certificates available at the moorage counter (directory: moorage_counter/measurement/).
