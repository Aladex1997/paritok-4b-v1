GOVERNING AUTHORITIES

All determinations must be grounded in the following governing documents:
- Standing Order 15-3: Governs skip file requirements. Section 3.2 requires a contemporaneous cover sheet plus a supporting record assembled on the day the slip was worked. Section 3.3 requires supporting records to be created contemporaneously (on the day the slip was worked). Section 5.1 names the skip event register as a District record. Per Appeal 2018-3, the burden of proof shifts to the District when a skip is not evidenced; if the District cannot produce a contemporaneous support record, the skip is upheld for the applicant.
- Resolution 2022-06: Governs billing rate adoption (effective August 1, 2022), billable length formula, and amendment verification requirements. Section 1.1 sets post-adoption rates. Section 4.3 requires amendments to be supported by certification. Section 1.4 governs billable length.
- Tariff Resolution 2019-14: Pre-adopted tariff framework (effective March 12, 2019). Rates differ from post-adoption rates. Used only for periods before August 1, 2022.
- Appeal 2018-3 Decision: Legal precedent establishing that the District bears the burden of proof for missed skips unless contemporaneous same-day proof is provided.

BILLING POLICY

Billable length = MAX(rounded-up check-in LOA from applicant register, posted slip length from slip inventory). Post-adoption rates apply to periods beginning August 1, 2022. Pre-adoption rates apply to periods before August 1, 2022. Billable basis is foot-month (length in feet multiplied by months billed).

Contemporaneous proof of eligibility means a cover sheet plus a supporting record (measurement certificate or equivalent) assembled on the day the slip was worked, as required by Standing Order 15-3 s.3.2-3.3.

QUALIFYING CRITERIA FOR EXCEPTION FLAGS

Exception flags must be assigned based on the following criteria - you must determine the correct category for each exception by analyzing the source data:
- Priority 1 (immediate board attention): Skip events without same-day proof per SO 15-3 s.3.2; LOA amendments without certification source and no downstream skip use; unresolved cases requiring legal review; late supporting records; invalid reason codes; any other case requiring immediate determination.
- Priority 2 (operational review): File/event identity mismatches where supplied file supports a different slip than registered; other skip exceptions with ambiguous documentation.
- Priority 3 (background tracking): Subledger arithmetic errors; minor reconciliation discrepancies; low-risk items requiring monitoring only.

You must classify each exception into the correct priority tier. The distribution is not predetermined - derive it from the data.
