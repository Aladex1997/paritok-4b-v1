precedent note -- Appeal 2018-3 (Auldbrook)

holding, as I use it in scoring: a skip stands or falls on the
file as it existed when the slip was worked. bare reason code =
conclusion, not record. register is the vessel of record; a
broker's listing for a boat the applicant doesn't own can't
supply a particular. remedy was prospective only because the
slip had already been accepted in good faith.

where it bites in our sample:
- the four cover-sheet-only files fail under 2018-3 directly.
  cover sheet = the bare code. no support = skip can't be
  sustained ON THE FILE. (merits may still be right, see
  WL-02211 ledger history, but the FILE fails.)
- WL-02319: support dated 3 days late. 2018-3 says the day
  worked controls. scored 0. JM argued for a grace period;
  nothing in 2018-3 or SO 15-3 gives one.

quote I keep using: 'the controlling moment is the working of
the slip, because that is the moment rank order is disturbed.'
