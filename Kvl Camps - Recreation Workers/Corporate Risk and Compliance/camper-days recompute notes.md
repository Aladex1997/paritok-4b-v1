# Camper-days / tenure-exposure recompute - method notes (WF3)

R. Delgado, 2026-06-24. Method only - numbers live in the workbook.

Camper-days: per season, per session, enrolled campers x waterfront days in the
session. Corrigwell sessions run 20 waterfront days (S1, S2) and 18 (S3) after
weather holds. 2022-2025 baseline.

Tenure exposure: count campers whose current band rests on a 2022-or-later Corrigwell
sign-off, weighted by consecutive summers classified under it. A 2022 nonswimmer who
returned four summers and now holds a swimmer band has four seasons of exposure even
though each season's test was fresh - the record chain is what is defective, not any
single test.

Prioritization for re-assessment: current 2026 campers first (they swim this summer),
then 2025 swimmers whose bands would gate 2027 enrollment, then the archive. Do NOT
attempt to re-test campers no longer enrolled - document and move on.

Sources: session calendar and enrollment workbook, classification rosters 2022-2026,
incident logs (to confirm zero welfare linkage - scope isolation holds).
